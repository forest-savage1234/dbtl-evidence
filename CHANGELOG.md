# Sequential releases

- **v0.1** — Identity, immutable revisions, sealed dependencies, atomic writes.
- **v0.2** — Retained content-addressed evidence bytes and tamper checks.
- **v0.3** — Domain contracts and atomic, strict CSV ingestion.
- **v0.4** — Construction roles, recursive lineage/impact queries, indexed acquisition checks.
- **v0.5** — Calibrated titer, declared context, exclusions, biological-unit summaries.
- **v0.6** — Frozen datasets, computation/source fingerprints, historical replay.
- **v0.7** — Correction findings, review dispositions, explicit recalibration/cohort refresh.
- **v0.8** — CLI, offline report, end-to-end synthetic ranking reversal, research survey.
- **v0.9** — Independent oracles, concurrency, raw-row binding, stronger audit, bounded path display, local benchmark.
- **v1.0** — Scoped research release, verified backup/restore, clean-package execution, complete contract, source/history bundle.

All releases are local research milestones. The final version does not denote
enterprise deployment readiness, validated biological results, or universal
resolution of the original problem.
