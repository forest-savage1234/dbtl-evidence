# v1.2 — bounded evidence pools

## Scope
Declarative, bounded measurement predicates and immutable snapshots of the
registered journal, including exclusions and candidates with unknown metadata.
Snapshots reconstruct their original watermark after independent results arrive.

## Challenges
New independent results, missing context, mismatches, superseded context,
forged membership/journal hashes and attempted executable/unbounded predicates.

## Limits
Coverage is only of the registered local history. No automatic discovery of
world literature or unimported laboratory files, and no industrial eligibility
is implied by inclusion. Pool-change review is the next gate.
