# v1.4 — quantity and estimator semantics

## Scope
Retained CSV values, units and pairing IDs are bound to logical source records.
Typed product/substrate-consumed, product/biomass, concentration and endpoint-
average productivity calculations require explicit denominator semantics.
Ratio-of-means and mean-of-paired-ratios are separate operations.

## Challenges
The independent 2.5 versus 2.0 estimator example; wrong biomass denominator;
missing/duplicate pairs; changed values/pair labels; corrupted source; zero
denominator; and exact mass/concentration/time unit conversion.

## Limits
Semantic basis and origin are declarations, not analytical or biological proof.
This does not modify Claim Review. No instantaneous-rate, carbon-yield, censored-
data estimator or propagated uncertainty is implied by these arithmetic results.
