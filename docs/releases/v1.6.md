# v1.6 — scoped genotype observations

## Scope

Structured source calls are retained with exact material and strain versions, UTC observation time, declared generation, method, clone/population scope and limitations. Evaluation checks named regions and a declared time/generation window. Measurement and sample identity assessments remain separate reviewer assertions. Legacy `asserted_verified` cannot substitute for this observation.

## Challenges

Tests exercise absent and unassessed observations, partial coverage, old generation/time, population versus clone, wrong material, discordant calls, arbitrary note rejection and altered evidence bytes. A correct call at one region never fills an unobserved region.

## Limits

This imports precomputed calls; it does not call variants, authenticate sequencing, verify a whole genome, establish population homogeneity or predict genetic stability. Time, generation, intended state and material linkage are declared metadata requiring external verification. Matching declared strings is scoped concordance only.
